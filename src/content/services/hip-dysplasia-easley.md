It occurs when the ball and socket of the hip joint do not fit together properly, leading to joint instability, pain, and eventually arthritis. Larger dog breeds are especially at risk, but hip dysplasia can affect pets of any size or age.</p><p>Early diagnosis and intervention are crucial. Left untreated, hip dysplasia can severely impact your pet’s quality of life, limiting their ability to run, play, or even walk comfortably.
