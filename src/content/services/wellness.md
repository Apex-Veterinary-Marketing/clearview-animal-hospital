      <h2>What to Expect During a Wellness Visit</h2><p>Every wellness visit at Clearview follows the same caring approach, so you and your pet know what to expect.</p><h3>A Conversation About Your Pet</h3><p>A wellness visit starts with a conversation. We’ll ask how your pet is eating, drinking, sleeping and playing, and whether you’ve noticed any changes at home.       <h2>Why Wellness and Preventative Care is Important for Your Pet</h2><p>Every pet benefits from preventive care, from puppies and kittens who are building immunity, to healthy adults who seem perfectly fine, to seniors whose needs change from year to year.</p><h3><strong>Catching Problems Early</strong></h3><p>Pets are good at hiding illness, and some problems show up in an exam or in the blood before they show up at home. Finding them early usually means simpler, more effective treatment.</p><h3><strong>Prevention Costs Less Than Treatment</strong></h3><p>Preventing disease is more convenient and more cost-effective than treating it after the fact. Vaccines and parasite prevention protect your pet from illnesses that can be serious or even life-threatening.</p><h3><strong>A Plan That Fits Your Pet</strong></h3><p>Your pet’s species, age, breed and lifestyle all shape what they need. Regular visits let us build and adjust a preventive plan around your pet as they grow and change.</p><h2>Signs Your Pet May Be Due for Wellness Care</h2><p>It’s time to book a wellness visit if your pet:</p><ul><li>Hasn’t been seen by a veterinarian in the last year</li><li>Is due for vaccines or a heartworm test</li><li>Has gained or lost weight</li><li>Is slowing down or acting differently</li><li>Has changes in appetite, thirst, skin or coat</li></ul><p>If your pet seems unwell between checkups, same-day appointments are available, so please give us a call.</p><h2>How Often Should Your Pet Receive Wellness Care?</h2><p>Most adult dogs and cats should have a wellness exam at least once a year, along with the annual bloodwork, fecal analysis and heartworm testing we recommend. Puppies, kittens and senior pets often need to visit more often. We’ll suggest a schedule that fits your pet’s age, health and lifestyle.</p><h2>Practice Highlights</h2><p>Here’s what you can count on when you bring your pet to Clearview:</p><ul><li><strong>Caring for Easley since 1958</strong>, trusted by families in Easley and across the Upstate for generations</li><li><strong>In-house laboratory</strong> for bloodwork and heartworm testing right here in our hospital</li><li><strong>Same-day appointments</strong> when something comes up between checkups</li><li><strong>Online booking and PetPage</strong> so you can request appointments and keep your pet’s records handy</li><li><strong>Payment plans available</strong> through Cherry and CareCredit</li><li><strong>Serving Easley and surrounding communities</strong> including Pickens, Clemson, Liberty and Greenville</li></ul>
