      <h2>What to Expect During Tumor Removal</h2><p>From the first exam to the lab results, here’s what happens when your pet has a lump removed.</p><h3>Exam and Testing</h3><p>We’ll feel the lump, note its size and location, and ask how long it has been there and whether it has changed.
