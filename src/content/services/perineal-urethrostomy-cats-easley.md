This surgery is most often recommended for cats suffering from recurrent urethral blockages, which can be life-threatening if not properly addressed. Male cats are particularly susceptible to urinary obstructions due to the narrow shape of their urethra. These blockages may be caused by urinary stones, crystals, or inflammation and can quickly lead to severe pain, kidney damage, or even death if untreated.</p><p>The primary goal of a perineal urethrostomy is to create a new urinary opening that is less likely to become obstructed, allowing your cat to urinate comfortably and safely. This surgery can be life-saving and helps prevent future emergencies, reducing stress for both cats and their families.
