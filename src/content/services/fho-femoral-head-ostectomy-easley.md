      <h2>FHO (Femoral Head Ostectomy) in Easley, SC</h2><p>Clearview Animal Hospital provides expert FHO surgery in Easley for dogs and cats experiencing hip pain or mobility challenges. Our experienced veterinary team is dedicated to your pet’s comfort and recovery.</p><h2>Understanding FHO (Femoral Head Ostectomy)</h2><p>FHO (Femoral Head Ostectomy) is a specialized orthopedic surgery that removes the head and neck of the femur bone, eliminating the painful bone-on-bone contact in the hip joint. This procedure is recommended for pets suffering from severe hip dysplasia, fractures, or chronic joint disease when other treatments cannot restore comfort or function.</p><p>By removing the damaged portion of the hip, FHO surgery allows the surrounding muscles to form a "false joint." This helps relieve pain and restores your pet’s ability to walk, run, and play with less discomfort. Most pets recover well and return to a more active lifestyle after surgery. FHO can be performed on both dogs and cats and is especially helpful for small to medium-sized pets.</p><p>Our veterinary professionals at Clearview Animal Hospital use advanced surgical techniques and compassionate care to ensure the best possible outcome for every patient.</p>
