      <h2>What to Expect During a Nutrition Consultation</h2><p>Nutrition conversations usually happen as part of your pet’s <a href="/services/comprehensive-physical-exams-easley">exam</a>. Here’s how we approach them.</p><h3>Weight and Body Condition Check</h3><p>We’ll weigh your pet and assess their body condition to see whether they’re at a healthy weight.</p><h3>Reviewing What Your Pet Eats</h3><p>We’ll ask what your pet eats in a typical day: their main food, treats, table scraps and any supplements. It helps to bring a photo of the food label or a list of everything your pet eats.</p><h3>A Tailored Feeding Plan</h3><p>We’ll look at the whole picture, including your pet’s age, breed, activity level, allergies and medical history. If your pet needs to lose or gain weight, we’ll suggest a realistic feeding plan and how much to feed. If your pet has a health concern such as digestive upset, heart health or urinary problems, we may recommend a diet designed to support that condition. We’ll also help you make sense of food labels so you can shop with confidence.</p><h3>Follow-Up Weigh-Ins</h3><p>Follow-up weigh-ins let us see what’s working and fine-tune the plan as your pet’s needs change.</p>

      <h2>Why Good Nutrition is Important for Your Pet</h2><p>What your pet eats every day affects their energy, weight, coat and long-term health.</p><h3><strong>A Healthy Weight</strong></h3><p>Extra weight raises the risk of joint pain, diabetes and other chronic conditions. The right diet and portions help your pet stay at a healthy weight.</p><h3><strong>Support for Health Conditions</strong></h3><p>Diet can play a big role in managing weight concerns, digestive issues, heart health and urinary problems.</p><h3><strong>The Right Food for Each Life Stage</strong></h3><p>Puppies, kittens, adults and seniors all have different nutritional needs. We help you adjust your pet’s diet as they grow and age.</p><h2>Signs Your Pet May Need Nutrition Advice</h2><p>Every pet can benefit from a diet check, but it’s especially helpful if your pet:</p><ul><li>Is overweight or underweight</li><li>Has a sensitive stomach or food allergies</li><li>Is moving from puppy or kitten food to an adult diet, or entering their senior years</li><li>Has been diagnosed with a condition that diet can help manage</li><li>Shows changes in appetite, coat quality or energy</li></ul><p>If you’ve noticed any of these, talk with our team at your pet’s next visit or give us a call.</p><h2>How Often Should Your Pet’s Nutrition Be Reviewed?</h2><p>We recommend reviewing your pet’s diet and body condition at every <a href="/services/wellness">wellness visit</a>, at least once a year. Pets on a weight-loss plan or a special diet often benefit from more frequent weigh-ins, and it’s a good idea to check in whenever your pet’s life stage or health changes.</p><h2>Practice Highlights</h2><p>Here’s how Clearview makes good nutrition easier:</p><ul><li><strong>Caring for Easley since 1958</strong>, with generations of Upstate pets who have grown up with us</li><li><strong>Plans for every life stage</strong>, guiding puppies, adults and seniors toward balanced, safe food choices</li><li><strong>Food delivered to your door</strong> through our <a href="https://clearvieweasley.covetruspharmacy.com/">online pharmacy, Covetrus Pharmacy</a>, which ships food and medications straight to your home</li><li><strong>Payment plans available</strong> through Cherry and CareCredit</li><li><strong>Serving Easley and surrounding communities</strong> including Pickens, Clemson, Liberty and Greenville</li></ul>
