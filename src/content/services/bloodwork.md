      <h2>Pet Bloodwork in Easley, SC</h2><p>Welcome to Clearview Animal Hospital, where our veterinary team uses bloodwork to look after the health of pets in Easley, Pickens, Clemson, Liberty and Greenville.</p><h2>Understanding Pet Bloodwork</h2><p>Your dog or cat can’t tell you when something feels off, and pets are very good at hiding illness. A small blood sample can tell us a great deal about what’s happening inside the body, often before any signs show up at home.</p><p>Bloodwork is one of the tests handled by our <a href="/services/laboratory">in-house laboratory</a> at Clearview Animal Hospital. A complete blood count (CBC) looks at red blood cells, white blood cells and platelets, while a blood chemistry panel checks how organs such as the liver and kidneys are working, along with blood sugar, proteins and electrolytes. Together, these results help us spot problems like anemia, infection, kidney or liver disease and diabetes, and they give us a baseline to compare against as your pet gets older.</p>

Here’s how a visit usually goes.</p><h3>An Exam and a Conversation</h3><p>Bloodwork usually starts with an exam and a conversation about how your pet has been doing. Normal results are reassuring and give us a helpful baseline.       <h2>Why Bloodwork is Important for Your Pet</h2><p>Bloodwork is useful for healthy pets and sick ones alike. It gives us a look inside the body that a physical exam alone can’t provide.</p><h3><strong>Early Detection of Hidden Disease</strong></h3><p>Conditions such as kidney disease, liver disease and diabetes often develop quietly. Bloodwork can reveal them early, when they are usually easier to manage.</p><h3><strong>Safer Anesthesia</strong></h3><p>Before surgery or a dental cleaning, bloodwork helps us check for problems such as anemia, infection or organ changes so we can plan anesthesia with your pet’s safety in mind.</p><h3><strong>A Baseline for the Years Ahead</strong></h3><p>Results from healthy visits give us a personal baseline for your pet. Comparing future results against it helps us notice small changes as your pet ages or while they take long-term medications.</p><h2>Signs Your Pet May Need Bloodwork</h2><p>We may recommend bloodwork as part of a wellness visit, before anesthesia, for senior pets or for pets on long-term medications. It’s also a good idea if your pet shows any of these signs:</p><ul><li>Vomiting or diarrhea</li><li>Drinking or urinating more than usual</li><li>Weight loss or a poor appetite</li><li>Tiredness or low energy</li><li>Just seeming “not right”</li></ul><p>If you notice any of these changes, call Clearview Animal Hospital. Same-day appointments are available when your pet isn’t feeling well.</p><h2>How Often Should Your Pet Have Bloodwork?</h2><p>For healthy adult pets, we recommend bloodwork once a year as part of the yearly wellness visit. Senior pets, pets with ongoing conditions and pets on long-term medications often need testing more often so we can catch changes early and adjust care. Sick pets are tested as needed to find the cause and track their recovery.</p><h2>Practice Highlights</h2><p>Why families trust Clearview with their pet’s bloodwork:</p><ul><li><strong>In-house laboratory:</strong> many blood tests are run right here, so you don’t have to wait on an outside lab for every answer.</li><li><strong>Same-day appointments:</strong> when your pet isn’t feeling well, same-day visits are available.</li><li><strong>Caring for Easley since 1958:</strong> a trusted local animal hospital for generations of Upstate pets.</li><li><strong>Family-focused care:</strong> you and your pet are part of the Clearview family.</li><li><strong>Payment options:</strong> Cherry and CareCredit payment plans can help you plan for the cost of care.</li></ul>
