faqs:
  - question: "What does a complete blood count (CBC) show?"
    answer: |-
      <p>A CBC looks at the three main types of blood cells: red blood cells, which carry oxygen; white blood cells, which fight infection; and platelets, which help stop bleeding. Changes in their numbers, size or shape can point to problems such as anemia, inflammation, infection, bleeding risk or bone marrow disease, and often guide what to test next.</p><p class="faq-source">Source: <a href="https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/complete-blood-count">VCA Animal Hospitals, Complete Blood Count</a></p>
  - question: "What does a blood chemistry panel check?"
    answer: |-
      <p>A serum biochemistry panel measures substances in the liquid part of the blood. It includes liver enzymes, kidney values such as BUN, creatinine and SDMA, blood sugar, proteins, minerals like calcium and phosphorus, and electrolytes such as sodium and potassium. Abnormal results can point to disease and help show which organ system is affected.</p><p class="faq-source">Source: <a href="https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/serum-biochemistry">VCA Animal Hospitals, Serum Biochemistry</a></p>
  - question: "Why does my pet need bloodwork before anesthesia?"
    answer: |-
      <p>Pre-anesthetic bloodwork checks for problems such as anemia, infection, clotting risk, and liver or kidney abnormalities. Some findings can be corrected first — for example, mild dehydration may be treated with IV fluids before surgery — while serious problems may lead your veterinarian to postpone the procedure until the condition is diagnosed and treated. The results also help guide the choice of anesthetic drugs.</p><p class="faq-source">Source: <a href="https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/bloodwork">VCA Animal Hospitals, Preanesthetic Bloodwork</a></p>
  - question: "My senior cat seems healthy. Why test her blood?"
    answer: |-
      <p>Cats are good at hiding illness, and older cats are more likely to develop disease. Wellness testing, including a CBC and a biochemistry profile, aims to find problems such as kidney disease, liver disease and diabetes early, when they are easier to manage, and to monitor conditions that are already being treated. Many owners pair this testing with the cat’s yearly veterinary visit.</p><p class="faq-source">Source: <a href="https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/wellness-testing-for-senior-cats">VCA Animal Hospitals, Wellness Testing for Senior Cats</a></p>
  - question: "Can bloodwork find kidney disease before my cat shows signs?"
    answer: |-
      <p>Often, yes. Chronic kidney disease is one of the most common diseases of senior cats, and early on it usually causes no visible signs. Blood tests measure waste products the kidneys normally filter, including BUN, creatinine and SDMA, and SDMA can rise earlier than creatinine. Earlier diagnosis and treatment are associated with better quality of life, which is why routine exams and bloodwork matter for older cats.</p><p class="faq-source">Source: <a href="https://www.vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/chronic-kidney-disease">Cornell Feline Health Center, Chronic Kidney Disease</a></p>
