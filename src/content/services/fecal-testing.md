      <h2>Pet Fecal Testing in Easley, SC</h2><p>Welcome to Clearview Animal Hospital, where our veterinary team uses fecal testing to help keep pets and families in Easley, Pickens, Clemson, Liberty and Greenville free of intestinal parasites.</p><h2>Understanding Fecal Testing</h2><p>Intestinal parasites such as roundworms, hookworms, whipworms and single-celled organisms like Giardia are common in dogs and cats, especially puppies and kittens. Many infected pets look healthy, while others have diarrhea, vomiting, weight loss or a dull coat. Some of these parasites can also be passed to people, so finding them early protects your whole family.</p><p>A fecal test checks a small stool sample for signs of these parasites. At Clearview Animal Hospital, fecal samples are sent out to an outside veterinary laboratory for testing, while many of our other tests are run in our <a href="/services/laboratory">in-house laboratory</a>. When the results come back, we’ll let you know what was found and recommend the right treatment if your pet needs it.</p>

      <h2>What to Expect During Your Pet’s Fecal Test</h2><p>Fecal testing is easy for your pet. There’s no needle and nothing uncomfortable. Here’s how it works.</p><h3>Collecting a Fresh Sample</h3><p>All we need is a small, fresh stool sample. Collect it as close to the appointment as you can, keep it as free as possible of grass, gravel or litter, and bring it in a clean container with a tight lid. If you can’t bring one, let us know and we’ll talk about options.</p><h3>Testing at an Outside Laboratory</h3><p>We send the sample to an outside laboratory, where it is checked for parasite eggs and other signs of infection.</p><h3>Going Over the Results</h3><p>Once we have the results, we’ll go over them with you and explain what was found.</p><h3>Treatment and Prevention</h3><p>If parasites are found, we’ll recommend treatment and may suggest a follow-up fecal test to make sure it worked. We’ll also talk about parasite prevention that fits your pet’s lifestyle.</p>

      <h2>Why Fecal Testing is Important for Your Pet</h2><p>Every dog and cat benefits from routine fecal testing. It’s a simple way to catch parasites that you can’t see.</p><h3><strong>Finding Parasites in Healthy-Looking Pets</strong></h3><p>Many pets with intestinal parasites show no signs at all. A fecal test can find an infection before it affects your pet’s health.</p><h3><strong>Protecting Your Family</strong></h3><p>Some intestinal parasites of dogs and cats can also infect people, especially children. Finding and treating them helps protect everyone in your home.</p><h3><strong>Making Sure Treatment Worked</strong></h3><p>When a pet has been treated for parasites, a follow-up fecal test helps confirm the infection is gone.</p><h2>Signs Your Pet May Need a Fecal Test</h2><p>Fecal testing is especially important for new puppies and kittens, newly adopted pets, pets that spend time outdoors or around other animals, and pets that aren’t on year-round parasite prevention. Call us if your pet has any of these signs:</p><ul><li>Diarrhea</li><li>Vomiting</li><li>Weight loss</li><li>A dull coat</li><li>Not growing as expected</li></ul><p>If you notice any of these signs, contact Clearview Animal Hospital and bring a fresh stool sample to your pet’s visit.</p><h2>How Often Should Your Pet Have a Fecal Test?</h2><p>For healthy adult pets, we recommend a fecal analysis at least once a year as part of the annual wellness visit. Puppies and kittens need several tests during their first round of visits, and pets with symptoms, a history of parasites, or a more outdoor lifestyle may need testing more often. We’ll suggest a schedule that fits your pet.</p><h2>Practice Highlights</h2><p>Why families choose Clearview for their pet’s preventive care:</p><ul><li><strong>Complete wellness care:</strong> fecal testing fits right into your pet’s yearly exam, along with bloodwork and heartworm testing.</li><li><strong>In-house laboratory:</strong> many other tests are run right here at our hospital.</li><li><strong>Caring for Easley since 1958:</strong> a trusted local animal hospital for generations of Upstate pets.</li><li><strong>Family-focused care:</strong> you and your pet are part of the Clearview family.</li><li><strong>Payment options:</strong> Cherry and CareCredit payment plans can help you plan for the cost of care.</li></ul>
